# Sugar Futures Research Sources

This reference defines preferred sources for research related to ICE White Sugar No. 5 futures.

Use the highest-quality and most direct source available. Do not treat all sources as equally reliable.

## Source Priority

Prefer sources in approximately this order:

1. Exchanges, regulators, governments, and official statistics
2. Sugar industry associations and crop agencies
3. Established commodity and financial news organizations
4. Specialist agricultural and sugar publications
5. Company reports and trading-house commentary
6. Analyst commentary
7. Social media, forums, Reddit, and other community sources

Social-media information may provide useful leads or market sentiment, but important factual claims should be corroborated whenever possible.

---

# ICE White Sugar No. 5

## ICE

Primary source for:

- White Sugar No. 5 contract specifications
- Contract months
- Expiry information
- Exchange notices
- Delivery information
- Futures market information

Domain:

`ice.com`

When discussing a futures price, identify the relevant contract month whenever possible.

Do not confuse ICE White Sugar No. 5 with ICE Raw Sugar No. 11.

---

# Brazil

## UNICA

Primary source for Brazil Center-South sugarcane industry data.

Domain:

`unica.com.br`

Prioritize for:

- Cane crush
- Sugar production
- Ethanol production
- Sugar/ethanol production mix
- ATR
- Crushing progress

Pay attention to whether data refers to a two-week period, cumulative crop year, or comparison with the previous season.

## CONAB

Brazilian government crop agency.

Domain:

`conab.gov.br`

Prioritize for:

- Sugarcane crop estimates
- Production forecasts
- Yield estimates
- Area estimates
- National and regional crop outlook

## Brazil Trade Data

Use official Brazilian trade statistics when available for:

- Sugar exports
- Export destinations
- Export volumes

Clearly distinguish shipment data from production data.

# India

Prioritize official Indian government announcements for:

- Sugar export policy
- Export quotas
- Ethanol policy
- Cane policy
- Government production estimates

Useful government domains may include:

`gov.in`

## ISMA

Indian Sugar & Bio-energy Manufacturers Association.

Use for:

- Sugar production estimates
- Crushing progress
- Industry commentary
- Ethanol diversion estimates

Where government and industry estimates differ, report both and identify their sources.

# Thailand

Prioritize Thai government and sugar-industry sources for:

- Cane crushing
- Sugar production
- Crop forecasts
- Export availability
- Government policy

Where reliable primary data is unavailable, corroborate specialist reporting with multiple sources.

# European Union

## European Commission

Domain:

`ec.europa.eu`

Use for:

- EU sugar production
- Beet acreage
- Agricultural policy
- Imports and exports
- Market statistics

Monitor major producing countries including France and Germany when relevant.

# United Kingdom

Prioritize official UK agricultural information and major industry participants for:

- Sugar beet acreage
- Crop conditions
- Beet yields
- UK sugar production
- Import requirements

Clearly distinguish UK information from EU data following Brexit.

# United States and Global Data

## USDA

Domain:

`usda.gov`

Prioritize for:

- Global sugar production
- Consumption
- Imports
- Exports
- Stocks
- Country balance sheets
- World sugar outlook

Pay attention to publication dates because USDA estimates may lag faster-moving crop developments.

---

# Positioning

## CFTC

Domain:

`cftc.gov`

Use Commitment of Traders data for positioning context when relevant.

Important:

ICE White Sugar No. 5 trades in London and should not be confused with US-listed Raw Sugar No. 11 positioning.

CFTC data for Raw Sugar No. 11 can still provide useful information about broader speculative positioning in the global sugar complex.

---

# Energy

Monitor:

- Brent crude oil
- WTI crude oil
- Gasoline
- Brazilian ethanol
- Ethanol parity

Energy markets can affect Brazilian mills' economic incentives between sugar and ethanol.

Do not automatically assume higher crude oil means higher sugar prices. Explain the actual mechanism and current Brazilian ethanol economics.

---

# Currency

Pay particular attention to:

## BRL/USD

The Brazilian real can affect incentives for Brazilian producers and exporters.

A weaker BRL can increase the local-currency value of dollar-denominated sugar exports, but the actual market effect depends on hedging, production economics, and other conditions.

Also monitor relevant movements in:

- USD index
- INR
- THB
- EUR
- GBP

when material.

---

# Weather

Monitor weather affecting:

- Brazil Center-South cane
- India monsoon and cane regions
- Thailand cane regions
- European sugar beet
- UK sugar beet

Separate:

- observed weather
- short-term forecasts
- seasonal forecasts
- crop-impact estimates

Do not treat a weather forecast as a confirmed crop loss.

# News Sources

Established financial and commodity news organizations can be used to identify current developments and market explanations.

When available, prefer reporting that:

- identifies its data source
- names the analyst or organization providing an estimate
- provides a publication date
- distinguishes facts from forecasts

Do not repeat a news organization's explanation for a price move as established fact unless the causal link is well supported.

---

# Social and Community Sources

Reddit, X, YouTube, forums, and other community sources can be useful for:

- identifying emerging discussion
- discovering potential developments
- understanding trader sentiment
- finding topics requiring further investigation

They should not normally be the sole evidence for:

- production figures
- government policy
- export decisions
- crop damage
- futures prices
- official statistics

Verify material claims against higher-quality sources whenever possible.

---

# Research Rules

For every material claim, try to capture:

1. Publication date
2. Event/data date
3. Source
4. Country or region
5. Relevant crop season
6. Whether the information is confirmed, estimated, forecast, commentary, or rumor

Be particularly careful with old information resurfacing on social media.

A recently published article may discuss data that is several weeks or months old. Distinguish the publication date from the date of the underlying information.

When sources disagree, report the disagreement rather than silently choosing one estimate.

The objective is not to collect the largest number of articles. The objective is to identify the most decision-relevant new information affecting ICE White Sugar No. 5 futures.
